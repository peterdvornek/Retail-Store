# 🛒 Retail Inventory Demand Analysis

## 📦 About the Dataset

This dataset provides **synthetic yet realistic daily data** for analyzing retail store inventory demand. It contains over **73,000 rows** spanning multiple stores and products, with attributes that reflect real-world retail operations.

For this project, we focus exclusively on **Store 1**.

### 🔑 Key Data Features

- **Date**: Daily records from `[start_date]` to `[end_date]`
- **Store ID & Product ID**: Unique identifiers for stores and products
- **Category**: Product categories (e.g., Electronics, Clothing, Groceries, etc.)
- **Region**: Geographic region of the store
- **Inventory Level**: Stock available at the beginning of the day
- **Units Sold**: Units sold during the day
- **Demand Forecast**: Predicted demand based on historical trends
- **Weather Condition**: Daily weather impacting sales
- **Holiday/Promotion**: Indicators for holidays or promotional events

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

1. **Total Sales by Category**
   - _Why?_ Identify whether any product category has a competitive edge in units sold.

2. **Monthly Sales by Category**
   - _Why?_ Analyze sales trends over time to uncover seasonal patterns or volatility.

3. **Growth Ratio**
   - _Why?_ Help inventory managers optimize purchasing decisions based on sales performance.


---

## 📊 Key Findings

### 1. Total Sales by Category
- In both **2022 and 2023**, no single category dominated sales.
- Each category contributed roughly **20% of total units sold**, suggesting balanced demand.

### 2. Monthly Sales by Category
- In **2022**, categories like **Electronics, Clothing, and Furniture** showed greater volatility in monthly sales.
- In **2023**, fluctuations were less extreme, but the same categories continued to exhibit larger swings.

### 3. Growth Ratio
- **Toy and Grocery** categories experienced a decline in unit sales across both years.
- These trends suggest a need to **reassess inventory levels** and investigate potential causes (e.g., market shifts, pricing, consumer behavior).
